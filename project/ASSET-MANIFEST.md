- **Generation:** Higgsfield MCP, model requested **`nano_banana_2`** (2k). The service reports the backend as `nano_banana_flash` for these jobs; this is recorded as the actual model.
- **Higgsfield project:** "CleanUp Mini Games – game assets" (`21f4df55-05f7-4bef-a8c3-a68d58b70adb`).

## Checks per asset (all PASS)

- **Transparency:** every cutout has both fully transparent and fully opaque pixels. Edges were reviewed on light, dark and pitch backgrounds (`reference/review/`). No halo, colour spill or checkerboard; the translucent sprayer tank was kept.
- **Margins / cropping:** every object lies fully inside its master with ≥ 120 px of clear margin at 2k. Nothing is cropped.
- **Ball states alignment:** fitted centres within ±3 px and radii within ±1 % of the clean master at 2k. All states are registered to one 1024 canvas (radius 440). The stacked layers are clipped to the clean silhouette.
- **Working points:** marked and verified visually (chisel blade tip, sprayer and lance nozzle tips, bristle contact lines, cloth centre). Stored in `assetMeta.js` and used by `src/content/tools.js`.
- **Proportions at game size:** tools are sized in object-local units relative to the ball (chisel ≈ 0.55 × ball diameter, as in the reference). Verified in-game on phone and desktop screenshots (`project/screenshots/step3/`).
